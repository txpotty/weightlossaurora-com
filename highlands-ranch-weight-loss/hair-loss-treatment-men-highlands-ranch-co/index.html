    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Highlands Ranch, CO

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Highlands Ranch

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. In genetically sensitive men, DHT gradually miniaturizes follicles, starting at the temples or crown and moving inward. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil works on a separate track, lengthening the growth phase.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. Results are strongest where some hair remains, since long-bald areas seldom regrow. Earlier is better. Patchy bald spots, scalp pain or rapid loss call for a dermatologist visit. The provider checks your photos for the typical pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Highlands Ranch

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride, when used, is a daily capsule.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. By month six, loss typically slows and density starts to improve. Expect the full effect near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Highlands Ranch

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Scalp itch is the usual topical complaint. Oral minoxidil sometimes brings body hair or fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. They also ask directly about past depression, current sexual health and any plans to have children soon, since those shape which medicine is chosen. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in Highlands Ranch

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Surgery is an option for some.
